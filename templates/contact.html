Frequently asked questions

How soon will you reply?

Most messages get a response within 1–2 business days. Urgent route issues are prioritized.

Can I reschedule or cancel a pickup?

Yes. Go to Schedule → select your booking → Edit or Cancel. Changes are allowed up to 2 hours before your window.

What qualifies as bulky waste?

Sofas, mattresses, wardrobes, tables, appliances. Choose Bulky Waste at booking and attach a clear photo so we dispatch the right vehicle.

Do you take e-waste or batteries?

Yes, but pack separately and label clearly. For items with hazardous components, follow the on-screen instructions when scheduling.

Do I need to be at home during pickup?

Not for regular bins if they are accessible. For bulky items, ensure access (gate codes, concierge notes) and keep pathways clear.

What is the pickup window?

Drivers may arrive ±15 minutes around your chosen time due to batching and traffic. Live ETA updates are visible on Track.

My pickup was missed—what should I do?

Open Complaint and choose “Missed pickup.” We’ll prioritize a make-good visit and credit your account where applicable.

How do Eco Points work?

You earn points for verified recycling, on-time set-outs, and photo confirmations. Redeem discounts from the Rewards area in your dashboard.

Is there a fee for stairs or long carries?

For bulky items, a handling fee may apply beyond 2 flights of stairs or 25 m carry distance. Estimates are shown before you confirm.

Which containers are accepted for recycling?

Paper/cardboard, clean plastics, metals, and glass. Please rinse containers and flatten boxes; keep plastic bags out of the recycling bin.

How do I add special instructions?

Use the Additional Instruction box while scheduling. You can edit these notes until 2 hours before the window.

Can I donate usable items?

Yes—select Donate from your dashboard for accepted items and partner drop-off schedules. Some pickups can be combined.

What happens to photos I upload?

They’re used only to plan routing, equipment, and proof of service. Photos are stored securely and follow our retention policy.

I didn’t get the activation email.

Check spam/junk, then use “Resend activation” on the login page. If still missing, contact us here and we’ll help verify manually.

How is my data used?

Only to provide and improve service. We never sell personal data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
