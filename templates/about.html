About Us

Our story started with something simple yet real. One of our teammate's mother, being busy with her job, often couldn't manage waste on time—causing garbage to pile up. Around the same time, while returning from university, we noticed an old wooden table left abandoned by the roadside, without an owner, waiting to be picked up by anyone.

These small but eye-opening moments made us realize how unmanaged waste creates bigger problems in our daily lives and our cities. That's when we decided to take action.

From July 2025, we began working on a digital waste management platform that helps residents schedule pickups, track complaints, and encourage recycling. Our goal is simple—turn waste into a managed process, reduce pollution, and build cleaner, smarter, and more sustainable cities.

How We Work

1

Request / Schedule

Residents submit a pickup request or report an issue through our platform.

2

Assign & Notify

The system automatically assigns the nearest driver or collector and sends instant notifications.

3

Pickup & Track

The driver collects the waste, while residents can track the progress in real time.

4

Confirm & Update

Once the task is completed, the resident receives confirmation through the app.

5

Recycle / Dispose

The collected waste is delivered to proper disposal or recycling facilities for sustainable management.
