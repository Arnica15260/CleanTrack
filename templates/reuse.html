What can I donate?

Almost anything in usable condition.

  • Furniture, appliances, kitchenware, toys, books
  • Clothing/shoes (clean), electronics (working)
  • Large/bulky? Just add a photo for routing
🛡️
Safety & privacy
  • Meet in a visible place if possible
  • Share only what you’re comfortable with
  • Keep paths clear for movers/van access
🚚
Pickup tips
  • Disassemble big items if you can
  • Label fragile parts and bag small pieces
  • Take a photo so takers know what to expect
🔔
After someone accepts
  • Coordinate pickup via email/phone
  • Mark it “Donated” on your dashboard afterward
