What counts as recyclable?

  • Paper & cardboard (flatten boxes)
  • Metal cans, glass bottles, PET/HDPE plastics
  • Keep batteries & e-waste separate

Weighing tips

  • Zero the empty bag or subtract its weight
  • Round to one decimal (e.g., 1.8 kg)
  • Log one material per entry; choose Mixed recyclables if it’s mixed
